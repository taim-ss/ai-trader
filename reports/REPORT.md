# Corrected backtest report: monthly ETF momentum

Status: historical research only; no broker fills or paper-trading performance. Not financial advice. Regenerated by `scripts/run_backtest.py`.

This corrected simulation supersedes the earlier report: the earlier engine held weights constant each day, implying uncharged daily rebalancing, and filled at the signal close.

## Setup

- Cached Yahoo adjusted-close data: 3,473 aligned rows, 2013-01-02 to 2026-10-22. No data redistribution.
- Universe: SPY, QQQ, IWM, EFA, EEM, TLT, GLD, XLE, XLK, XLV, XLF, XLI, XLP, XLU. Same strategy and universe as the earlier run; no tuning.
- Signal: 252-session lookback, latest 21 sessions skipped; top 3 positive scores. Primary cap 15% per name; 33% sensitivity only. Remaining allocation is cash.
- Month-end signal uses data available at that close; execution is at the next observed session close. Existing holdings receive that session's return before execution. This is a close-only approximation, not a next-open fill simulation.
- Dollar holdings drift between trades; rebalances include the cost of restoring target weights. Costs are 10 bps slippage + 15 bps FX per actual traded dollar value; commission 0, all assumptions. Post-cost equity and traded value are solved together. Cash earns 0%.

Daily hit rate is the fraction of all daily account returns above zero, including cash days; it is not a trade win rate. Annual turnover is gross buys plus sells divided by pre-trade equity, summed and divided by calendar years (two-sided turnover). Sharpe/Sortino use 252 annualisation and a zero risk-free rate.

## Limits and interpretation

- One historical path and a current surviving ETF universe; not a clean out-of-sample test. The rule design followed literature review. This correction is not a new holdout test.
- The first 252 sessions are strategy warm-up in cash while the buy-and-hold benchmarks are invested from the start. This comparison includes that opportunity cost.
- All account returns are USD. A flat FX fee does not model EUR/USD returns. EUR cash interest, spreads, taxes, and observed broker fills remain unmodelled.
- Adjusted closes approximate reinvested distributions and splits; they are not executable quotes. Same-day execution bias is reduced by delaying fills, but close fills remain assumed.
- SPY and URTH are ETF benchmark proxies, not direct index returns; benchmarks exclude transaction costs, whereas the strategy includes the stated assumptions.
- The backtest does not simulate the paper engine's order caps, daily-loss/cooldown stops or drawdown kill switch. It is strategy research, not a broker execution replay.
- Yahoo's public chart endpoint is unofficial; cached history lacks a point-in-time revision archive.
